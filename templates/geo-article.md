## Writing Checklist

Before publishing, verify:

- [ ] **First paragraph** contains a statistic or data point
- [ ] **Every section** opens with a definitive claim, not filler
- [ ] **Key paragraphs** are standalone-quotable (can be copy-pasted)
- [ ] **At least 3 statistics** appear in the article
- [ ] **At least 1 original insight** (your own data, opinion, or research)
- [ ] **FAQ section** exists with 3+ questions
- [ ] **Author byline** with credentials and link to author page
- [ ] **Sources** listed at the bottom with links
- [ ] **Schema.org JSON-LD** included (BlogPosting or Article)
- [ ] **Meta description** is 40-60 words with primary keyword
- [ ] **Title** includes primary keyword and a data point
- [ ] **Tables** used for comparisons (at least 1)
- [ ] **Bold statements** used for key takeaways
